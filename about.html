About ADHMAT

What the tool is for, who it serves, and why it is moving to a web platform.

Purpose

The Africa Digital Health Maturity Assessment Tool (ADHMAT) was developed by Africa CDC so that Member States can systematically assess the maturity of their digital health systems at national and sub-national level, and the digital readiness of individual health facilities.

It is a core instrument under Africa CDC’s Digital Transformation Agenda, which aims to build a region- and country-owned digital health ecosystem with real-time, trusted data flows from community and facility level to national and continental decision-making.

Three assessment levels

N

National

373 questions across five domains, plus a supplementary Digital Payment Systems module.

S

Sub-National

327 questions, focused on what applies below national level.

F

Facility

A rapid digital health readiness assessment for individual facilities.

From workbooks to a web platform

ADHMAT has run as Excel workbooks and, in some countries, offline forms. That worked for early rounds, but it does not scale to 55 Member States.

Limits of the current approach

  • Data sits in separate files with no shared continental view
  • Results are gathered and analysed by hand
  • Version-control errors and delays are likely
  • Member States have no persistent place to keep and update their data

What the web platform is for

  • National assessments completed in country portals
  • Member States own and update their own assessment
  • Standard results that can be compared across countries
  • An evidence base for the State of Digital Health Report

Who it is for

The webinar series invites the Directors of Digital Health, Directors of Primary Health Care and Directors of RMNCH of the Ministries of Health of all 55 African Union Member States. Directors are encouraged to nominate national ADHMAT focal points.

National focal point

One designated official who coordinates ADHMAT for the country.

Ministry-led team

An assessment team led by the Ministry of Health that holds the evidence.

Three deliverables

The national assessment, qualitative inputs, and a country case study.

Complementary evidence. The State of Digital Health Member State input questionnaire adds qualitative evidence to ADHMAT results: recent progress, implementation experience and context that scores alone cannot show.

Safeguarding Africa’s Health

The Africa Digital Health Maturity Assessment Tool (ADHMAT) is an initiative of the Africa Centres for Disease Control and Prevention, Digital Health and Information System Division.
