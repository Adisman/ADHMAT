FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the tool, the roadmap and the dashboards.

What is ADHMAT?

The Africa Digital Health Maturity Assessment Tool, developed by Africa CDC, helps Member States assess the maturity of their digital health systems at national and sub-national level, and the readiness of individual facilities.

Who completes the assessment?

A Ministry-led assessment team, coordinated by a designated national focal point. Directors of Digital Health, Primary Health Care and RMNCH are invited to nominate the focal point.

What do Member States need to deliver?

The national ADHMAT assessment, the qualitative inputs, and a country case study.

How many questions are there?

373 in the National tool and 327 in the Sub-National tool (version 2.5). Digital Payment Systems is a supplementary module.

What are the maturity levels?

L1 Nascent (below 40%), L2 Emerging (40–54%), L3 Established (55–69%), L4 Advanced (70–84%) and L5 Optimized (85% and above).

Is the overall score used to rank countries?

No. The domain profile is the headline result. The overall score is a secondary summary and is not intended for ranking.

Do I need to record evidence?

Yes. For each Yes answer, record the evidence source, date and verification status.

What are the key dates?

Webinar 1 on 28 October 2026; 13 November 2026 for Member States from the Kigali convening to complete their assessment; the continental workshop on 19–20 November 2026; the review window for the first draft opens on 22 December 2026; and the Report launches at the African Union Summit in February 2027. See the roadmap.

Is the continental dashboard showing real results?

No. It uses generated sample data to test the design for all 55 Member States, and it is labelled as such.

Where is my data stored when I use the national dashboard?

The dashboard reads the CSV file in your browser. This website does not send it to a server. Do not load patient-level or confidential data.

Where can I get the assessment workbooks?

Africa CDC shares them with national focal points. They are not hosted on this site.

Is this the official Africa CDC website?

Not yet. This is a prototype prepared for review.
