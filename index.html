What ADHMAT does

One consistent method to measure digital health maturity, understand the result, and decide what to do next.

Assess

National, Sub-National and Facility tools measure maturity across five domains: Digital Environment, Architecture & Data, Applications, Analytics, and AI & Emerging Technology.

Understand

Results show a domain profile first, mapped to one of five maturity levels, with completeness reported alongside. The overall score is a secondary summary.

Act

The dashboard highlights strengths, weaknesses and priority areas, and the findings feed the State of Digital Health Report in Africa.

Five maturity levels

Each domain score maps to a level. These bands are the ones used in the ADHMAT tool.

L1Nascentbelow 40%
L2Emerging40–54%
L3Established55–69%
L4Advanced70–84%
L5Optimized85% and above
