Roadmap to the State of Digital Health Report

The steps from the first webinar to the launch of the first Annual Report on the State of Digital Health in Africa.

  1. 28 Oct 2026

    Webinar 1

    ADHMAT platform and roadmap to the Report

  2. 13 Nov 2026

    Kigali convening Member States

    Complete the national ADHMAT assessment, qualitative inputs and case study

  3. 19–20 Nov 2026

    Continental workshop

    Onboarding of all Member States on ADHMAT, qualitative data collection, consensus on the Report outline

  4. 22 Dec 2026

    Review window opens

    First draft of the Report

  5. Feb 2027

    Report launch

    At the African Union Summit

Monthly webinars run from November 2026 to February 2027 to support Member States through the assessment and review.

Webinar 1

Introducing Member States to the Africa Digital Health Maturity Assessment Platform and the roadmap to the State of Digital Health Report in Africa.

When

Wednesday, 28 October 2026, 4:00 PM EAT (Addis Ababa time). Virtual webinar.

Who should attend

Directors of Digital Health, Directors of Primary Health Care and Directors of RMNCH of the Ministries of Health of all 55 African Union Member States. Directors are encouraged to nominate national ADHMAT focal points to join them.

The webinar will

  • Introduce the web-based ADHMAT platform and how national assessments are carried out in country portals
  • Explain what Member States need to do: designate a national focal point and a Ministry-led assessment team, then complete the national assessment, qualitative inputs and a country case study
  • Present the roadmap to the Report
  • Open the floor to questions and identify the support Member States need before the November workshop

Register on Zoom

What Member States need to do

1. Designate a focal point

One named official coordinates ADHMAT and the Report for the country.

2. Set up a Ministry-led team

A team led by the Ministry of Health that brings together the people who hold the evidence.

3. Complete three deliverables

The national ADHMAT assessment, the qualitative inputs, and a country case study.

Safeguarding Africa’s Health

The Africa Digital Health Maturity Assessment Tool (ADHMAT) is an initiative of the Africa Centres for Disease Control and Prevention, Digital Health and Information System Division.
