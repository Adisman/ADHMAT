How an assessment is completed

The documented flow for a national assessment.

  1. Collect answers and evidence

    Answer each question Yes, No or N/A. For each Yes, record the evidence source, date and verification status.

  2. Calculate sub-domain scores

    A % score is calculated for each of the seven sub-domains.

  3. Convert to a maturity level

    Each % score maps to one of five levels.

  4. Read the domain profile

    The average of the applicable sub-domains gives each domain score. This profile is the headline result.

  5. Report completeness

    Maturity is always shown together with how much of the tool was answered.

The overall score is the average of the five domain scores. It is a secondary summary and is not intended for ranking countries.
